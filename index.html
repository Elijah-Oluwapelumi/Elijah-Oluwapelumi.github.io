Professional Profile

I am Oluwapelumi Elijah, a Geospatial Scientist with a First-Class degree in Remote Sensing and Geoscience Information Systems from the Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), Nigeria. My work combines Earth Observation, GIS, spatial analysis, and Python to explore environmental and societal challenges, with experience spanning environmental monitoring, coastal dynamics, mineral exploration, and geospatial intelligence.

I am interested in using geospatial data and analytical methods to better understand environmental change and support evidence-based decisions. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on research and applied projects, and I am keen to pursue graduate studies that will deepen my skills in Earth Observation, geospatial data science, and environmental modelling.
